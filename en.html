How to install

Three steps, under a minute.

  1. Download the fileClick the download button and wait for Dawiyat-Setup-0.4.0.exe to finish downloading.
  2. Open the fileIf a blue Windows box titled "Windows protected your PC" appears, click "More info", then "Run anyway".
  3. Install and startChoose an install location or keep the default, then open Dawiyat from the desktop and enter your name.
Why does Windows show that box? The app is new and does not have a code-signing certificate yet, so Windows lists the publisher as "unknown". The app needs no internet and sends no data; your progress stays on your device.
